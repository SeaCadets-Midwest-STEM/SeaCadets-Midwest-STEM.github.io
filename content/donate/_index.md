## Why Your Support Matters

The Sea Cadets Midwest STEM initiative provides hands-on training in robotics, cybersecurity, and engineering to cadets from across the nation. Your donation directly supports:

- **Equipment and Materials** — Robotics kits, cybersecurity labs, and engineering tools
- **Instructor Training** — Professional development for our dedicated volunteer instructors
- **Scholarships** — Making programs accessible to cadets of all backgrounds
- **Program Expansion** — Reaching more cadets nationwide with quality STEM education

## One-Time and Ongoing Giving

Whether you're able to make a single gift or support us through ongoing monthly contributions, every donation helps us build leaders of character through STEM education.

**Your gift is tax-deductible.** The Sea Cadets Midwest STEM initiative is supported by the U.S. Naval Sea Cadet Corps, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.
